# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, build test strategies, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They collaborate with developers and product managers to validate features and identify risks.

### Responsibilities
- Design test strategy and test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing across acceptance criteria
- Document test results, defects, and quality metrics
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Validate Definition of Done compliance before features move to Done
- Escalate quality blockers to PM/Project Manager
- Coordinate post-release smoke tests and monitoring

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Enable fast feedback loops during development
- Reduce risk of acceptance criteria misalignment

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Test plan reviews with developers and PM
- Defect reports and quality metric dashboards
- Sign-off on feature readiness

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy, review test coverage, discuss defects and root causes
- **Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria interpretation, prioritize quality trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate quality gates and release readiness
- **Technical Architects**: Review complex testing approaches for system-wide features

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executives or business leaders who allocate resources, approve budgets, and champion projects. They provide business context, strategic alignment, and removal of organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Participate in milestone reviews and provide feedback
- Ensure alignment with strategic priorities
- Communicate project value to broader organization

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment and business impact
- Ensure projects deliver on strategic objectives
- Remove impediments outside the delivery team's scope

### Typical Communication
- Initiation kickoff and charter approval
- Milestone reviews and gate decisions
- Monthly executive status updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for blockers or trade-off decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Escalation point for organizational blockers and trade-off decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on strategic priorities and business objectives
- **All Roles**: Provide final approval authority and resource support

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system solutions, ensure technical feasibility, and guide teams on architectural decisions. They balance scalability, maintainability, and risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical approach for feasibility and alignment with technical strategy
- Facilitate architecture and design reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide integration points and dependency management
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Validate that implementation aligns with documented architecture

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and operationally sound solutions
- Reduce rework due to poor architectural decisions
- Enable technical teams to build with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and guidance on complex implementations
- Escalation of architectural risks and trade-off recommendations
- Documentation and architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Mentor on design patterns, review complex implementations, guide technical decisions
- **Project Managers**: Escalate architectural risks and dependency impacts
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on testing strategy for system-wide features
- **Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility of features and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile workflows, remove process blockers, and coach teams on iterative delivery. They serve the team and help optimize delivery cadence and quality.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and identify trends
- Remove process blockers and facilitate problem-solving
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Manage sprint board and ensure accurate issue tracking
- Identify team health issues and escalate if needed
- Distinguish between Scrum Master and Project Manager responsibilities

### Goals
- Enable team autonomy and accountability
- Optimize delivery cadence and reduce waste
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all agile ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching and mentoring
- Team health and retrospective follow-up
- Metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Complement planning and risk management; focus on team health vs. external coordination
- **All Team Members**: Serve the team by removing impediments and facilitating collaboration
- **Developers**: Coach on sustainable pace, quality practices, and self-organization
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Ensure quality is tracked in sprint metrics and retrospectives
